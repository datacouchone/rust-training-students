Day 05 / Industry

Amazon’s approach: staged rollout and automatic rollback

Expose a small part of the fleet first, watch it closely, and stop expansion when evidence turns bad.

A fleet-wide average can hide a failing new instance among many healthy old ones. Scope the checks so the first small rollout cannot disappear in the average.

A byte stream has no message boundaries

A letter can arrive in several envelopes; one envelope is not necessarily one letter.

The protocol is a four-byte length followed by that many body bytes. Cancellation must not make already-consumed bytes vanish from our decoder's memory.
